# Third-Party and User-Supplied Game Content

NUKE HOUR is an open-source engine shell. Public builds contain GPL-licensed
community support artwork from the OpenRA Red Alert 2 and Romanov's Vengeance
projects, plus NUKE HOUR project-original artwork. The exact files, hashes,
source revisions, and licenses are recorded in
`docs/legal/public-bits-provenance.json`.

The bundled support artwork is not a substitute for the retail games. A
byte-level comparison against same-named files in lawfully owned RA2/YR MIX
archives found no identical retail files in the reviewed support-art set.

Command & Conquer: Red Alert 2 or Yuri's Revenge retail game data.

names, artwork, audio, maps, models, and other game data are trademarks or
copyrighted works of their respective owners. Electronic Arts does not endorse
or sponsor this project.

Players must lawfully obtain the original games and import their own local files.
Imported content remains on the player's device. Public builds accept data-only
packages and reject executable code, external assemblies, and mod extensions.
